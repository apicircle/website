This Refund Policy forms part of our [Terms of Service](/terms). It explains when you can get your money back for an API Circle subscription and how to ask.

**It does not reduce any right to cancel, withdraw or obtain a refund that the law of your country gives you.** Where the law, or the refund policy of our reseller Paddle, gives you more, the more generous right applies.

## 1. Summary

- **Free trial:** paid plans currently start with a 30-day free trial. Cancel before it ends and you are not charged anything.
- **14-day money-back guarantee:** if you are not happy, ask within 14 days of your **first payment** for a subscription and we will refund it in full.
- **Annual renewals:** ask within 14 days of an annual renewal payment and we will refund it in full.
- **Monthly renewals:** these are not refundable, but you can cancel at any time to stop future payments. Your plan stays active until the end of the month you have paid for.
- **If something we sell does not work:** if a paid feature does not work as described and we cannot fix it within a reasonable time, we will refund you for the affected period.
- **How:** use the "Request refund" link in your Paddle receipt, or email us at {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}.

## 2. Who processes refunds

Our order process is conducted by our online reseller Paddle.com. Paddle.com is the Merchant of Record for all our orders. Paddle provides all customer service inquiries and handles returns.

All refunds are paid by Paddle to the payment method you used, in the currency you paid in. Your purchase is also covered by Paddle's [Buyer Terms](https://www.paddle.com/legal/buyer-terms) and [Refund Policy](https://www.paddle.com/legal/refund-policy). You may ask either Paddle or us for a refund. We will work with Paddle so that you receive what this policy promises.

## 3. Free trials

If your plan starts with a free trial, you give a payment method at checkout but nothing is charged during the trial. If you cancel before the trial ends, you pay nothing and there is nothing to refund.

If you do not cancel, the trial converts automatically into a paid subscription when it ends, and your first payment is taken. That first payment is covered by the money-back guarantee in section 4.

## 4. Our 14-day money-back guarantee

You can ask for a full refund of the **first payment** for a subscription within **14 days** of that payment. This is the first payment after a free trial ends, or your first payment if there was no trial.

- The guarantee applies to monthly and annual plans, wherever you live, and you do not need to give a reason.
- When we refund your first payment, we cancel the subscription and your account returns to the free plan. Workspaces above the free limit are **locked, not deleted**, and you can unlock them by subscribing again.
- The guarantee applies once per person or organisation.

## 5. Renewals

**Annual renewals.** If your annual subscription renews and you did not mean it to continue, ask within **14 days** of the renewal payment. We will refund it in full and cancel the subscription.

**Monthly renewals.** Monthly renewal payments are not refundable, except where section 7 or the law requires a refund. You can cancel at any time and will not be charged again. Your plan stays active until the end of the month you have paid for.

**Price changes.** We give at least 30 days' notice before a new price applies to your subscription. If we failed to give you that notice before a renewal was charged at a higher price, we will refund the difference, or the whole renewal payment if you ask within 14 days.

## 6. Team seats and plan changes

**Added seats.** Adding seats to a Team plan is charged immediately for the rest of the billing period. If you added seats by mistake, tell us within **14 days** of the charge. We will refund the charge for any added seats that have not been assigned to anyone or activated.

**Removed seats.** Reducing your seat count takes effect from your next renewal, so the current period is not refunded.

**Upgrades.** Moving to a higher plan charges the difference for the rest of the current billing period. During a free trial, nothing is charged. If you upgraded by mistake, tell us within **14 days** of the charge. We will refund it and return you to your previous plan.

**Cancelling or moving to a lower plan.** The change takes effect at the end of your current billing period. We do not give partial refunds for unused time, except as set out in this policy or where the law requires.

## 7. When something does not work

If a paid feature of the Services is unavailable or does not work as described:

1. Tell us at {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}.
2. If we cannot fix it within a reasonable time, we will refund the fees for the period in which you could not use it.
3. If the problem is serious enough that you want to end your subscription, we will refund the unused part of the period you have paid for.

## 8. Your statutory rights

**European Union, EEA, United Kingdom and Switzerland.** If you are a consumer, you have a right to withdraw from a purchase within 14 days, as set out in Paddle's [Buyer Terms](https://www.paddle.com/legal/buyer-terms). For subscriptions with a free trial, a 14-day withdrawal period starts again when the trial ends and the first payment is taken. For annual subscriptions bought in the United Kingdom, a further 14-day period applies at each renewal. We honour these rights in full. The money-back guarantee in section 4 is in addition to them.

**India.** If you are a consumer in India, you have the rights given by the Consumer Protection Act, 2019. You may also raise a complaint with our Grievance Officer (section 11).

**Everywhere else.** You have the rights your local consumer law gives you. If they are more generous than this policy, they apply.

## 9. How to request a refund

Any of these works:

- **Through Paddle:** open the receipt email Paddle sent you and choose the refund link, or go to [paddle.net](https://paddle.net), find your order and select **Request refund**.
- **Through us:** email [{{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}](mailto:{{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}) from the address on your account. Include your Paddle order or receipt number if you have it.

We will acknowledge your request within 2 business days and tell you the outcome within 5 business days. Once a refund is approved, Paddle usually processes it within 14 days. Your bank or card provider may take a further 5–10 business days to show it.

## 10. Chargebacks and misuse

**Please contact us before disputing a charge with your bank.** We can usually resolve problems faster than a chargeback can. If a payment is disputed while we are resolving it, we may suspend the related subscription until the dispute ends.

Where the law allows, we may refuse refund requests that show a pattern of misuse. An example is repeatedly subscribing, using a paid plan and requesting refunds. This never limits your statutory rights.
